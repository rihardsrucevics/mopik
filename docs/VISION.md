# Mopik — vision (far backlog)

**The rider, 2026-09-28: "Mopik jākļūst par Waze adventure motobraucējiem."**
First thoughts, not a plan; nothing here is scheduled. Kept so that today's
decisions don't close these doors.

## Where it goes
- **Plan and ride.** Mopik keeps planning rides super easily, and also
  becomes a **native app that navigates** — turn-by-turn on the ride it
  planned, not only a GPX to take elsewhere.
- **Collects what riders see, like Waze.** Riders report problems and risks
  on the way (closed gate, washed-out track, deep sand, a fallen tree,
  police, a dog) — see backlog 42 for the first step (report unrideable
  places, remembered by routing).
- **See other riders nearby** — to avoid running into each other on narrow
  forest tracks, and to find company.
- **Get help when needed** — a rider down or stuck can call for help from
  riders nearby / share location with friends.
- **More social than Waze** — Mopik is for like-minded people: shared rides,
  following friends, riding together.
- **Seasons and gamification** — season challenges, badges to earn
  (regions, TET sections, forest km, sights visited), maybe leaderboards.
- **Your bike's colours** — a theme that matches the rider's motorcycle.

## Principles that already hold and must keep holding
- No guessing: reports are explicit, dated, attributable facts.
- Honest durations; say when something is impossible.
- Nothing that encourages riding together with alcohol or tobacco.
- Privacy: seeing other riders and sharing location are opt-in, never default.
